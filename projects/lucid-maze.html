Lucid Maze

Date: Aug 2025

Duration: 4 Days

Home

Lucid Maze is a 2D side-scrolling platformer with a maze for you to explore. Fight your way through waves of monsters with makeshift weapons. Huff some gas to keep yourself sane–or not. Cleanse the place or find an exit; the choice is yours. An innovative hallucination system (WIP) that turns the spaceship into a nightmare to navigate, and makes it harder to read the notes left by the crew. Manage your sanity to be able to decipher logs and navigate the world, but run too low, and you'll faint. The truth is just out there, waiting for you.

The game is a psychological sci-fi platformer, developed during the Women's Game Jam in August 2025 with the theme "Nothing is as it seems." The game was created by a 5-member team over the course of 4 days, my primary responsibility as a Unity programmer was to implement the main gameplay, design the levels, add VFX and also implement art, UI, music and sound.

Our team conceived the idea where you awake on a derelict space station, air heavy with an unfamiliar haze, thoughts scattered like debris. The crew is nowhere to be found; only monsters roam the shifting hallways. Doorways vanish. Paths you swore existed are gone. You don't know how you got there, but you know what you have to do. The gas keeping you alive is scarce. When you run low, you see clearer, but it's not the same space station anymore. The notes scattered around by someone who knows what’s up become easier to read, but do you want to know what they feared?

Game Features

Enemies - These are monsters blocking your way, and they took over the scientists who were running the experiment. They are grotesque, terrifying creatures trying to get their cursed hands on you. Avoid them or fight them, but remember that not everything is as it seems.

Player - You’re not sure why, but you remember, in the haze before awakening here, someone calling you like that. You’re trapped in an unreliable, ever-changing maze. You like the gas, but when you run low on it, your mind becomes clearer, and you can think better. You're here with a mission, and you'll complete it, no matter what it takes, no matter what blocks your path.

Scaling:

Currently the game has four levels, therefore to expand the game, we'd add more levels with more codex pieces to find and tougher monsters, adding to the difficulty.

Responsibilities:

1. Programming

  • Player: Character movement, health, damage and attack, animation
  • Enemies: AI implementation, movement,health,damage and attack, animation
  • Physics and collision detection: For characters, enemies and objects throwing
  • Debugging and optimization

2. Level Design

  • Enemies: Stategic placement with damage and health adjustment for added difficulty
  • Rooms: Designing and layout for the rooms with different themes and features
  • Gas: Designing and placement for gas away or near enemies for player health recharge
  • Props arrangement - Objects eg chairs and lockers, for user guidane and room identification

3. Asset implementation

  • Art and animation
  • Music and sound
  • User Interface (UI): animations and transitions

4. VFX

  • Adding the camera distortion effect when you interact with the gas
