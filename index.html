About

について

I’m a gameplay programmer and developer focused on building interactive systems that feel intuitive, responsive, and purposeful. My work spans Unity and C#, where I design mechanics, state-driven systems, and connected gameplay experiences, as well as web technologies and HTML5 frameworks like Phaser. I’m drawn to the space where structure meets play—where systems quietly support the experience without getting in the way of it.

I’m drawn to systems that feel effortless to the player. The kind where logic is complex behind the scenes, but the experience remains simple and intuitive. Whether it’s managing transitions between game states, refining moment-to-moment interactions, or optimizing performance, I focus on creating experiences that feel cohesive, responsive, and intentional.

Currently open to full-time roles and select freelance collaborations.
