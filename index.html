About

pnm

I'm a gameplay programmer with 3+ years building interactive systems in Unity (C#) and Phaser (JS/TS) — from state-driven gameplay loops and enemy AI to UI flow and data architecture. My recent work spans a solo-built mobile release on the Play Store, a co-developed 2D metroidvania, and educational, research-backed titles built with NGOs and citizen-science partners. I care most about the layer of a game players never consciously notice: clean state management, responsive controls, and performance that holds up.

I’m drawn to systems that feel effortless to the player. The kind where logic is complex behind the scenes, but the experience remains simple and intuitive. Whether it’s managing transitions between game states, refining moment-to-moment interactions, or optimizing performance, I focus on creating experiences that feel cohesive, responsive, and intentional.

Currently open to full-time roles and select freelance collaborations.

Wahenga

Wahenga is an African sci-fi with a solar-powered twist set in recognisable future East Africa, after global ecological collapse. You are an energy scavenger adventuring through a ruined world. The wealthy reside above the smoke clouds, the rest in polluted lowlands. Free a host of Ancestral beings (the Wahenga) from their cursed state. After a chance encounter with a friendly Ancestral being, it’s on you to explore and ultimately restore your polluted homeland.

🔧 My Contribution
